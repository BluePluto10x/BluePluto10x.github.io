### Responsibilities

- Leading a team of over 20 engineering students to design, build, and test control solutions for the competitive electric racing motorcycle.
- Focused on wirelessly obtaining real time systems data from the motorcycle during its operation. The data includes physical motorcycle characteristics such as motor rpm, speed, inertial measurement unit data, and battery cell temperatures. The data also includes the electrical characteristic min/max cell voltage, average state of charge, DC bus current, and auxiliary state of charge.
- Designing and developing an executable GUI to visually represent transmitted data. Simple, clean visual representation of the data allows the team to monitor critical motorcycle metrics during an event.
